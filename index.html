OUR STORY

From our kitchen
to your table.

EMBER & OAK began with a simple idea — create a place where beautiful food feels effortless and every guest feels genuinely welcome.

Our kitchen celebrates seasonal produce, thoughtful techniques and flavours inspired by places we love. From slow-cooked classics to fresh plates designed for sharing, everything is prepared with intention.
